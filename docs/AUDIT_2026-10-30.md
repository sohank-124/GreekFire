# Audit started October 30, 2026

This audit builds on the existing, uncommitted preparation work. Historical experiment entries and earlier reports are retained. Their numbers do not validate the revised engine.

Initial source findings:

- The column-name membership bug had already been repaired with `hasproperty`. Its explanatory comment incorrectly blamed a DataFrames self-rename bug; the actual issue is comparing Symbols with string column names.
- Feature generation requires future labels and omits the last five observations. Missing outcomes therefore affect the candidate universe before ranking.
- Stock calendars are intersected with SPY, so five stock observations can span more than five market sessions.
- The volatility ratio called 10/60 actually uses at most 20 observations. Regression residual momentum sums intercept-fitted residuals and is nearly zero by construction.
- Acquisitions and different share classes are mapped to successor prices. Membership accuracy and security identity cannot be certified from the local CSV alone.
- The manual blacklist excludes entire historical securities based on later observations. Index removal is incorrectly described as delisting in some diagnostic helpers.
- Sector caps have a fallback that breaks them. Position-cap normalization can break the requested cap.
- Returns exit at each fifth close and enter at the following open. Deducting only weight changes omits liquidation and repurchase costs for this cash-between-windows convention.
- Metrics silently replace invalid returns with zero, omit failed steps, use raw mean returns as excess returns, and hardcode annualization and risk-free assumptions.
- Primary visualization exports assume 9% annual benchmark growth. Holdings indices are saved after their source table has been reordered.
- HMM and short-loss clipping are experimental and disabled; neither establishes an executable risk-control strategy.

The implementation and validation record is in `VALIDATION_2026-10-30.md`. Full market-data certification, delisting payouts, liquidity and an untouched evaluation period require evidence outside this repository.

Implemented corrections also retain raw volatility for risk sizing, reject duplicate price dates, repair join-order alignment on missing sessions, use strict sector caps, retain cash under infeasible position caps, preserve holdings indices after sorting, and share metric arithmetic between runners. The accidentally populated `.graphifyignore` contained an old Backtest source copy; it is preserved in `archive/graphifyignore_legacy.txt` and replaced with actual generated-directory exclusions.
